# Refund & chargeback draft (owner legal review required)

This is **not** final legal advice.

## Website posture (recommended)

- Digital coins and theme licenses are generally **final sale** once delivered/consumed.  
- Premium subscription cancels prospectively; prior billing periods are not refunded except where required by law or processor rules.  
- Duplicate charges, unauthorized payments, and failed entitlement delivery are handled case-by-case.

## What a website cannot promise

- Cardholders may still open disputes.  
- PayPal may reverse funds.  
- Local consumer law may override site wording.

Owner must review with counsel before live payments.
