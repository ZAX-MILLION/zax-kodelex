# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: July 20, 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how Zax Million uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels.

## How we use them

- **Essential:** login sessions, security, load balancing, cookie consent storage.
- **Preferences:** theme, language, and similar settings.
- **Analytics (if enabled):** understanding traffic and feature usage to improve the Service.
- **Marketing (if enabled):** only with consent where required.

## Your choices

You can control cookies through our preference tools (when shown) and through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may break sign-in or core features.
