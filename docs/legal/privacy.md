# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Zax Million ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Service.

**You provide:** account details (such as email and username), profile settings, support messages, and payment-related information processed by payment providers.

**Collected automatically:** device/browser data, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, usage events, cookies or similar technologies (see [Cookie Policy](cookies.md)), and security logs.

**From partners:** payment processors and infrastructure providers may share transaction status or fraud signals needed to operate the Service.

## How we use information

We use information to operate and improve the Service, authenticate users, process purchases, prevent abuse/fraud, comply with law, and communicate about the Service.

## Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we may rely on contract performance, legitimate interests (security, product improvement), consent (where required for cookies/marketing), and legal obligations.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

## Retention

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, security, and accounting requirements. You may request deletion of your account subject to lawful retention needs.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Your choices & rights

Depending on your region (for example GDPR/CCPA-style laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact **contact@zaxmillion.com**.

You may also manage cookie preferences where our cookie tools are available.

## Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children.

## International transfers

If data is processed in other countries, we take steps designed to protect it as required by applicable law.
