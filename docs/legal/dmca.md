# DMCA / Copyright Policy

**Last updated: July 20, 2026**

Zax Million respects intellectual property rights and expects users and self-hosting operators to do the same.

## Our position

- Unauthorized copyrighted content is not allowed.
- We respond to valid copyright notices.
- Repeat infringers may lose access.

## Notice of claimed infringement

If you believe material on a Zax Million-operated site infringes your copyright, send a notice that includes:

1. Your physical or electronic signature (or that of an authorized agent).
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
3. Identification of the allegedly infringing material and information reasonably sufficient to locate it (URLs help).
4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

## Counter-notification

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification with the information required by applicable law (for U.S. users, 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)). Upon a valid counter-notice, we may restore material unless the complainant seeks a court order within the statutory period.

## Self-hosted copies

If you run your own installation of this software, **you** must designate your own contact for copyright complaints and handle takedowns for your deployment. Do not send notices about third-party self-hosted sites to ZAXMIllion@proton.me unless that site is actually operated by us.

Knowingly submitting a materially false copyright notice or counter-notice may expose you to liability.
